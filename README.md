# 🎭 Ark of Art

> **Discover. Create. Connect. Inspire.**

Ark of Art is an open-source platform designed to connect artists and creative communities worldwide. It helps artists discover competitions, workshops, showcases, performances, festivals, auditions, exhibitions, and networking opportunities across every artistic discipline.

Whether you're a dancer, musician, singer, actor, theatre artist, painter, photographer, filmmaker, or any other creative professional, Ark of Art aims to become the central hub for finding opportunities and building meaningful connections.

---

# 🌍 Vision

To build the world's largest community-driven platform where artists can easily discover opportunities, collaborate globally, and showcase their talent without geographical barriers.

---
